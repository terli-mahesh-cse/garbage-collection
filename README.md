# 🌿 Garbage Collection

**Spot it. Post it. Clean it together.**

A community-powered clean-city platform where citizens photograph garbage spots, pin them on a live map, join volunteer cleanup drives, and help municipal authorities keep neighbourhoods clean.

## 🔗 Live Link

**👉 [https://garbage-collection-rose.vercel.app](https://garbage-collection-rose.vercel.app)**

Open it on any phone or desktop browser. It is a Progressive Web App, so you can also install it to your home screen.

## 🎯 What It's For

Garbage piles up in public places because problems go unreported and volunteers have no easy way to organise. This platform connects citizens, volunteer groups and municipal officers in one place:

1. **Spot & Snap**: Take a photo or video of garbage. The app captures the GPS location and street address automatically.
2. **Pin to the Live Map**: Post the report in seconds. Neighbours and officers see it on the map and can upvote it to prioritise cleanup.
3. **Clean Together**: Join Sunday and holiday drives, check in with a QR pass, earn points and download a certificate.

Map pins are colour-coded: 🔴 Reported, 🟠 In progress, 🟢 Cleaned.

## 🧭 How to Use It

| Who | What to do |
|-----|-----------|
| **Citizen** | Go to **Report**, add a photo, confirm the location, choose the garbage type and post. Then open **Events** to RSVP for a drive. |
| **Volunteer** | Join a community group, RSVP for a drive, show your QR pass at check-in and download your e-certificate. |
| **Municipal Officer** | Open the **Authority** dashboard, pick your ward, review open hotspots, assign sanitation teams and mark spots as cleaned. |

## ✅ Advantages

- **Fast reporting**: A report takes seconds, with automatic GPS and address detection.
- **Real-time live map**: Everyone can see what is open, in progress or cleaned, and filter by waste type and severity.
- **Community action**: Volunteer groups and Sunday drives turn complaints into actual cleanups.
- **Municipal collaboration**: Officers get a ward-level dashboard to assign teams, track resolution and export CSV reports.
- **Privacy-friendly**: A built-in blur tool masks faces and number plates before posting.
- **Motivating**: Points, a growing community tree, a leaderboard and downloadable certificates reward participation.
- **Awareness built in**: A waste-segregation guide, quizzes and safety rules for hazardous and medical waste.
- **Multilingual**: English, Hindi (हिन्दी) and Telugu (తెలుగు).
- **Works anywhere**: Installable PWA with an offline fallback, mobile-first and free for citizens.
